How an RGB asset lives on Arkade

Arkade gives bitcoin instant off-chain payments. RGB gives bitcoin private, client-side assets. Here the two meet: the asset rides on an Ark VTXO, moves as fast as Arkade, and every receiver checks it themselves.

Try each path for real in the Playground: the guided demo runs all of them in about two minutes.
